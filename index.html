FREEWHEEL
the road is failing · and you are what’s failing it
This is the last working season on the mountain road. You haul down it, against four others who want the same run, from the snowline to the sea.

Every load you take down wears out the line you drove. The fast road gets greasier and narrower each time you use it, and then one run it is simply gone — chained off, collapsed, under water. There are eight runs in a season and the mountain will not last all of them.

So the choice is never only which way is quickest. It is how much road you are willing to spend to get there, and what you are willing to carry: an overload pays half again and breaks the road under you almost twice as fast.
