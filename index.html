FREEWHEEL
no engine · height is your fuel
Once a year the mountain closes and the whole town becomes the track. No motors — only what gravity lends you, and only until you spend it.

Hold SPACE to tuck: low, fast, cheap. Let go as the road compresses under you — when the load meter passes 1 g — and you pump speed out of the hill itself. Stand up anywhere else and you are just a sail.
